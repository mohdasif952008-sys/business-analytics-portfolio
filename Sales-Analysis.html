Executive Summary

For May 2026 the business generated SAR 156,508 across 24 active selling days at an average of SAR 6,521/day. Revenue leans on PET bottles and retail customers, demand peaks mid/late week, and the field-team commission cost stayed at 3.74% of sales.

↑

Lean, predictable cost-to-serve

Total field commission was SAR 5,847 — 3.74% of net sales. Drivers carry SAR 4,957, helpers SAR 701, supervisors SAR 190.

≈

Two route archetypes are running under one P&L

The top route salesmen sit in a narrow revenue band. The real story is efficiency: Hujatul Islam earns SAR 333 per customer from 100 accounts, while Manzoor earns SAR 102 per customer across 293 stops — two different route models.

!

Revenue is concentrated

PET is 73.4% of bottle volume, the top 10% of customers drive 53.1% of revenue, and Saturday + Sunday deliver 40.9% of weekly sales.

Daily revenue and the weekly demand pattern. A strong pre-weekend stocking spike should drive fleet and inventory planning.

◆

Build routes around the peak

Saturday and Sunday are the demand engine. Weight your strongest drivers, extra helper support, and full inventory loading toward these days.

◷

Use the soft day for service

Wednesday is the weakest weekday. Use the slack for collections, bottle-return reconciliation, contract visits, and vehicle maintenance.
